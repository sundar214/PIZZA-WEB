Our craft

Slow dough. Fast fire.

Our oven has burned oak every day since 2001. The dough takes two full days to rise; the bake takes ninety seconds. That tension — patience and violence — is where flavour lives.

  1. 01

    Hand-stretched, never rolled

    Rolling kills the air pockets. Stretching keeps the leopard-spotted, pillowy cornicione.

  2. 02

    48-hour cold ferment

    Slow rise, deep flavour, easy digestion. Good dough cannot be hurried.

  3. 03

    480° oak fire, 90 seconds

    Char, chew and smoke in perfect balance — the oven floor never drops below inferno.

  4. 04

    Finished off the flame

    Fresh basil, Sicilian olive oil, flaky sea salt. No shortcuts, ever.
